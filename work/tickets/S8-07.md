# S8-07 Tree build that is fast on the card (results bit-identical)

Sprint 8. Depth: deep, fast turnaround. No card access. Deadline: card binaries within 60 minutes of start.
Start from main (has S8-01 timers and S8-03); read `.work/S8-02` (parallel tree patch, identity checker) and reuse
what helps; do not edit `.work/S8-02`.

## Why
Card, large case (20,000 bodies, 111 builds, 240 threads): serial `nbMakeTree` 13.19 s; S8-02's parallel tree
70.4 s (x86 12 threads: 1.34 s -> 3.61 s). The force walk is 13.0 s, so the tree must drop to ~1 s for the card to
dominate. We do not know where S8-02's time goes.

## Do
1. Sub-phase timers inside `nbMakeTree` (under `NBODY_PHASE_TIMERS`): `nbNewTree` (free-list rebuild),
   `expandBox`, bucketing/partition, insertion, `hackCofM`, `threadTree`, `hackQuad`, and malloc calls/bytes.
   Print as PHASE lines.
2. Three card variants, all with timers, static integrator, chunk 4, scalar force, parallel quad, built into
   `build/s8/tree-{serial,cofm,pool}/milkyway_nbody.mic`:
   - `serial`: upstream tree (timers only), to split today's 13.2 s.
   - `cofm`: serial insertion, parallel `hackCofM` + `hackQuad` over the subtrees below depth 3 (same octant order).
   - `pool`: full parallel build as S8-02, but with cells from one preallocated contiguous pool (no malloc or
     free-list walk per step; per-thread slabs by index), bucketing by a parallel key computation plus a stable
     counting sort (no recursive copies), and no OpenMP region inside a loop over levels.
   Pick what removes the cost you find in step 1 on x86; explain in one line per variant.
3. Identity: S8-02's every-step tree identity check (or your own) on small/medium/large at 1 and 12 threads for
   `cofm` and `pool`; `scripts/compare.py` bit-identical vs the references in `.work/S7-01/runs/force-chunk/`.
4. x86 PHASE lines (12 threads) for the three variants; `knc_lint` on the card binaries; print the card_run commands
   (large case, window-script form as in S8-02's `build/s8/card-commands.sh`).

## Accept
- Sub-phase timings, identity and compare output, binaries, commands in the Engineer report, plus the Effort line.
