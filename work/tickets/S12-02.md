# S12-02 Adopt exact (correctly rounded) vector log/atan (blocked on GCC-KNC M6)

Sprint 12 (backlog; blocked). Depth: deep.

## Why
GCC-KNC confirmed a correctly rounded 8-lane log/atan is feasible and bit-identical to crlibm by definition (quick
double-double phase with FMA, per-lane rounding test, rare accurate fallback; several times faster than scalar
crlibm per value). It is a multi-week clean-room library item in GCC-KNC's deferred vmath contract (S12-20, M6),
proposed to the owner. When it exists, it unlocks the star-setup integrand (`fun`, ~34 s of 48 s on core-small on the
card) and the NFW term of S12-01.

## Do (when the library lands)
Use it in S10-05's specialized (block 1) integrand and in S12-01; prove bodies byte-identical (core-small, full
117676) and likelihood bit-identical; measure on core-small and the full 117676 on the card.
