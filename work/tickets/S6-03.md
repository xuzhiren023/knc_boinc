# S6-03 Vector gravity: vectorize sqrt, division and the quad terms

Sprint 6. Depth: deep. Starts after S6-02 is merged. No card access.

## Settled since S6-01
GCC-KNC's V8DF division and sqrt expansion (vrcp23ps/vrsqrt23ps seeds + FMA refinement, gcc-knc S10-09) is correctly
rounded in all rounding modes with IEEE flags: proof in `gcc_knc/docs/research/knc-div-sqrt.md` section 3, card check
2026-11-12 (`gcc_knc/tests/regress/knc-v8df-divsqrt.c`: 268M random operands each plus midpoint, Markstein, exponent,
special and masked sets, bad=0). The FMAs are inside the expansion, so `-ffp-contract=off` does not apply to them;
the result equals IEEE div/sqrt. `docs/gcc-knc-flags.md:107-108` was stale (gcc-knc lead, 2026-11-16). So S6-01's
`#pragma GCC novector` on that stage is no longer needed. FMA contraction of *source* expressions stays off.
