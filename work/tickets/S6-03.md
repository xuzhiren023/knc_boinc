# S6-03 Vector gravity: vectorize sqrt, division and the quad terms

Sprint 6. Depth: deep. Starts after S6-02 is merged. No card access.

## Settled since S6-01
GCC-KNC's V8DF division and sqrt expansion (vrcp23ps/vrsqrt23ps seeds + FMA refinement, gcc-knc S10-09) is correctly
rounded in all rounding modes with IEEE flags: proof in `gcc_knc/docs/research/knc-div-sqrt.md` section 3, card check
2026-11-12 (`gcc_knc/tests/regress/knc-v8df-divsqrt.c`: 268M random operands each plus midpoint, Markstein, exponent,
special and masked sets, bad=0). The FMAs are inside the expansion, so `-ffp-contract=off` does not apply to them;
the result equals IEEE div/sqrt. `docs/gcc-knc-flags.md:107-108` was stale (gcc-knc lead, 2026-11-16). So S6-01's
`#pragma GCC novector` on that stage is no longer needed. FMA contraction of *source* expressions stays off.

**Disassembly.** The final linked `nbGravity8` has 813 instructions and zero
x87 add/sub/mul/div/sqrt. All arithmetic, including both monopole divisions,
quad inverse/phi divisions and sqrt, uses vectors. It contains one
`vrsqrt23ps`, eight `vrcp23ps`, 28 `vfnmadd231pd`, and 32 `vfmadd231pd`.
Those FMA instructions are the compiler's div/sqrt refinements. The remaining
x87 opcodes are data movement or the scalar cell-opening comparison:

```text
fldz 1; fstpl 26; fldl 29; fxch 2; fcompl 1; fnstsw 1;
fld1 2; fstl 1; fld 1; fstp 1.
```

The fld/fst/fxch instructions pack guarded pointer-loaded scalar values and
unpack results; `fcompl`/`fnstsw` compare the vector-computed unsoftened distance
against a cell criterion. They do not perform gravity arithmetic. Evidence is
in `twin/vector/evidence.json`, `conversion.json`, `vectorizer.txt`, and the
regenerable `nbGravity8.disassembly.txt`. A final re-extraction from the packaged
binary was checked equal to the complete function used by every twin group;
no instruction substitution, hot-region cut or costed stub was used. Example
from the final disassembly:

```text
40ada2: vrsqrt23ps {sae},%zmm1,%zmm1
40ada8: vcvtps2pd %ymm1,%zmm1
40adae: vmulpd {rn-sae},%zmm1,%zmm1,%zmm12
40adbc: vfnmadd231pd {rn-sae},%zmm12,%zmm11,%zmm9
40ade5: vfmadd231pd {rn-sae},%zmm13,%zmm9,%zmm12
```
