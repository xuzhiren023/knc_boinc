# S8-04 knc-twin model of the two serial hot regions (tree insertion, mixed-dwarf `fun`)

Sprint 8. Depth: light. No card access. Deadline: report within 60 minutes of start. Use the installed `knc-twin`
skill (read its SKILL.md and references/validation.md first; follow its workflow: capabilities, from_binary,
validate, functional where possible, bounds, simulate on `card-7120-measured`). No code changes to the app.

## Why
Window 12 card phase timers (S8-01, large case, 20,000 bodies, 111 tree builds): `nbMakeTree` 13.19 s on one card
thread = ~5.9 us per body per build (~7,300 core cycles at 1.238 GHz), vs 1.11 s on one laptop core. On the real
workunit 117676 the serial mixed-dwarf body generation (`nbody_mixeddwarf.c`, `dist_fun` -> `gauss_quad` -> `fun`)
takes ~4,560 s on the laptop. Before more card time we need per-call cycle predictions on one KNC thread, to model
the serial term and to check what S8-02 (parallel tree) and S8-03 (parallel quadrature nodes) can reach.

## Do
Binary: `.work/S8-01/build/s8/card-timers/milkyway_nbody.mic` (card scalar build; objdump from the K1OM binutils in
the GCC-KNC toolchain).
1. **Tree insertion.** Region `nbLoadBody` (inline into `nbMakeTree` if so: then the insertion loop of
   `nbMakeTree`), with `nbSubIndex`/`nbMakeCell` taken in (`--with-callee`) where not inlined. Input: real body
   positions, dumped from the x86 timers build at one step of the large case (a debug dump is fine: a small local
   patch in a scratch copy, not checked in), a 2,000-body subset in original order, and the root box from that step.
   Report simulate cycles per body, `bounds`, the limiting constraints (dependency chain, misses), status and
   result_class. Compare with the measured ~7,300 cycles per body (which also includes `hackCofM`, `threadTree`,
   `hackQuad`; say so).
2. **`fun`** (`nbody_mixeddwarf.c:364`) with its callees (`first_derivative`, `second_derivative`, `get_potential`,
   `get_density` for plummer and cored). If a callee uses an operation the twin does not support (for example an
   x87 transcendental or a crlibm routine), stop and report it as unsupported with the mnemonic; do not invent a
   stub cost. Inputs: the core-small / 117676 dwarf parameters from the Lua (`.work/S7-02/runs/real/inputs/`), three
   radii spanning one `gauss_quad` call. Report cycles per `fun` call or the unsupported list.
3. A short table in the Engineer report: region, unit, predicted cycles, bound, measured (where known),
   result_class, assumptions, unsupported.
